What Is Loyverse BigCommerce Integration?

Loyverse BigCommerce Integration connects your Loyverse POS system with your BigCommerce online store. It helps synchronize important business information, including products, inventory levels, and orders, between both platforms.

Instead of manually updating product information in two different systems, retailers can use an integration solution like SKUPlugs to streamline data synchronization and reduce repetitive tasks.

Why Do Retailers Need to Connect Loyverse with BigCommerce?

Retailers selling through physical stores and online channels need accurate inventory information. Without proper synchronization, a product sold in-store might still appear available on the website.
